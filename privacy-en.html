Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What data we collect

  • Account data: your email, username and password (the password is stored hashed by Supabase and we never see it).
  • What you post: posts, comments, summaries, reels, messages, questions and answers, and your profile photo.
  • Study activity: minutes, the exams you add, and your settings.
  • If you sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub we only receive your name and email from them.

Why we collect it

So your account works, your content reaches the people you allow, and you receive notifications. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who sees what

  • Your name, photo and posts are shown according to the Privacy setting in your account (everyone, followers only, or nobody).
  • Private messages are visible only to you and the other person.
  • Your email is not shown to other users.
  • Uploaded files and images have a direct link, so anyone who has the link can open them.

Artificial intelligence

The AI features (assistant, quizzes, summaries, card generation and the moderators' report check) run on Google Gemini. The text you write or choose for these features is sent to Google to be processed, without your name or account data. Google may use such text to improve its services under its own terms. Do not write personal information in these features.

Each user has a daily usage limit. Answers can be wrong and are no substitute for your book or teacher. When a moderator presses "AI check" on a report, only the reported content's text is sent, and the final decision is the moderator's, not the AI's.

New posts, reel titles and summary titles/subjects are checked automatically by rules inside our own database (abusive words, self-harm phrases, phone numbers and emails, promotional links, repeated posting). Nothing is sent to any outside service. Anything suspicious is only flagged for moderators, who decide; the system never deletes anything by itself.

In the assistant and the smart study timetable you can choose to upload a photo, take one with the camera or attach a PDF (up to 3 files). They are sent to Google Gemini together with your question so it can read them and answer. Photos are shrunk on your device before sending and are not stored by us. Do not upload anything with personal data or other people's faces.

If the optional AI moderation (Google Gemini) is turned on, only the text is sent to Gemini for the check; no images, videos, files or names.

Cookies and on-device storage

The site itself uses no tracking or advertising cookies. We keep your login session, language, theme and some settings on your device (localStorage) so the site can work; they are removed when you sign out or clear your browser data.

Some external services load with the page and may see your IP address or set their own cookies: Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection at sign-in), Google Fonts (fonts) and jsDelivr (the Supabase library). If you sign in with Google, GitHub or Discord, their own privacy policies apply.

You can block third-party cookies in your browser settings, but captcha protection may then fail and stop you from signing in.

Services we use

  • Supabase: database, storage and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: site hosting and CAPTCHA protection (Turnstile), and possibly visit statistics without tracking individuals.
  • Google Fonts: fonts.
  • jsDelivr: loads the Supabase library.

Your rights

  • Edit your name and profile details at any time from Settings.
  • Export your data from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings; your content is deleted with it.

Children

The site is for students. If you are under 13 you must ask a parent or guardian before signing up. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's account removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Reports and abuse

Any user can report a post or a user. We review reports and may remove content or suspend accounts.
